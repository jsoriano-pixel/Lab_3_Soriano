I lay in bed for a moment, listening to the quiet hum of the house. Sunlight slipped through the curtains and painted a bright rectangle across the floor. Then my stomach growled loudly enough to make the decision for me.
Breakfast.
I shuffled into the kitchen and found my dad already there, standing over the stove. A pan of scrambled eggs sizzled while two slices of bread popped out of the toaster.
“Morning,” he said.
“Morning,” I replied, still half asleep.
He slid a plate across the counter. On it were golden scrambled eggs, crispy toast, and a small pile of strawberries. I poured myself a glass of orange juice and sat down.
The first bite of toast was wonderfully crunchy. I spread some butter across the second slice, watching it melt into the warm bread. The eggs were soft and slightly salty, exactly how I liked them. I dipped a corner of toast into the eggs, and my dad immediately shook his head.
“You're going to get crumbs everywhere.”
I looked down at my plate. There were already crumbs everywhere.
“Too late.”
He laughed and returned to his coffee.
For a few minutes, neither of us said much. We simply ate while the morning slowly became louder. A car passed outside. Birds chirped from the backyard. Somewhere upstairs, a floorboard creaked.
When I finished, I leaned back in my chair, satisfied. The sleepy feeling that had followed me from bed was finally gone.
I picked up my glass and drank the last of the orange juice.
“Ready for the day?” Dad asked.
I considered the question.
My schedule was full, and I had plenty to do. But for the moment, everything felt strangely simple.
“Yeah,” I said. “I think I am.”
And with that, breakfast was over, leaving behind an empty plate, a few crumbs, and enough energy to face whatever the day decided to bring.
